## 1. Rakes in service: types, dimensions, capacity, speed, acceleration, power

### Takeaway
A Mumbai suburban coach is about 20.73 m long, 3.66 m wide and 3.81 m high, much wider than ordinary Indian coaches.
Trains are built from 3-coach units (1 motor coach + 2 trailers), so 12-car = 4 units. They run on 25 kV AC overhead
power everywhere since 2016. The fleet is Siemens-equipped rakes (MUTP-1, 2009 onward, mostly Central/Harbour), Bombardier rakes
(MUTP-2, 72 rakes), Medha rakes and BHEL/Medha AC locals, all built at ICF Chennai. Top speed is about 100 km/h for
Siemens rakes and 105–110 km/h for Bombardier/Medha rakes. The first non-AC rake with automatic doors arrived in April 2026.
238 new "Vande Metro"-type AC rakes are on order, first rake expected 2027. No reliable acceleration or braking figure was found.

### Inferences
- Train lengths over bodies (my calculation, couplers excluded): 12-car ≈ 12 × 20.73 = 249 m; 15-car ≈ 311 m; 18-car ≈ 373 m. Add roughly 0.5–1 m per coupling gap for the whole train (estimate).
- A 12-car rake is 4 units, so it has 4 motor coaches. If each motor coach carries one pantograph, which the unit formation suggests but no source confirmed, a 12-car train has 4 pantographs, one on every third coach.
- Game motion values (estimates): acceleration 0.5–0.8 m/s² (newer 3-phase rakes at the high end), top speed 100 km/h for Siemens-type and 105–110 km/h for Bombardier/Medha-type. 0→100 km/h then takes roughly 35–55 s.
- A crowd of 14–16 per m² against about 6 per m² "practical" explains people hanging out of open doors on non-AC rakes. Doorway crowds should be a key visual on non-AC trains but absent on AC and automatic-door rakes.
- A 12-car AC rake seats about 1,028, fewer than a non-AC rake's 1,161–1,168, so AC interiors can show slightly fewer seats and more standing room.

## 2. Coach order in a 12-car (and 15-car) train: ladies, first class, disabled, luggage, senior citizens; platform marking

### Takeaway
Coach positions are fixed per line and marked on every platform by painted boards or pillar colours. Exact current maps
per line were not found. Western Railway puts its ladies coaches toward the Virar end (an extra ladies section was added in 2022 in the
11th coach of a 12-car and the 14th of a 15-car). Central Railway's luggage compartment is in the 6th coach from the Mumbai (CSMT) end;
since 2025 it is being converted to a 13-seat senior-citizen compartment.
Platform boards often do not say whether a position is for a 12-car or a 15-car train.

## 4. Operations: dwell, doors, reversing, headway, signalling, staff, announcements, indicators

### Takeaway
Ordinary locals stop for about 20 s per station; AC locals stop close to 1 minute because of door cycles. Peak headway is about
3 minutes on Western Railway, and the signalling is near capacity. Automatic block signals are about 400–500 m apart with
red / yellow / double yellow / green aspects and an onboard Auxiliary Warning System enforcing speed caps. Plans for
Kavach 5.0 (cab signalling) target 2-minute headways. About 3,254 services run per day in total. Announcements and displays use
Marathi, Hindi and English. Platform indicators show a destination code, time, F/S and minutes to arrival; Western Railway coaches
have side displays showing destination, fast/slow and 12/15 car.

### Cited Findings
**Dwell and doors**
- A regular local halts for "nearly 20 seconds" at each station; AC locals halt close to a minute because of their doors, and Western Railway asked ICF to speed up door opening and closing — [TogetherVCan (aggregating a Hindustan Times report)](https://togethervcan.in/?p=2616)
- Door-closing delays on AC locals, alarm-chain pulling and trespassing caused delays of up to 20–25 minutes on the first day of a revised Western Railway timetable — [FPJ](https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/western-railway-services-delayed-up-to-25-minutes-on-first-day-of-revised-timetable)
- Public timetables round intermediate halts to 1 minute (e.g. Borivali–Churchgate slow) — [India Rail Info](https://indiarailinfo.com/train/pdf/borivali-churchgate-slow-local-90130/20022/845/11011)

**Headway, service volume, signalling**
- Peak headway on the Mumbai Central division (Western Railway): 3 minutes — [Western Railway / PIB presentation](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/userfiles/MTRC_PPT.pdf)
- Kavach 5.0 (cab-based protection) is meant to cut headway to about 2 minutes and raise daily services from 3,254 to nearly 4,000; the current signalling is close to capacity (July 2026) — [FPJ](https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/mumbai-local-trains-set-for-major-safety-upgrade-as-railways-develop-kavach-50-to-boost-capacity)
- An older Communication-Based Train Control (CBTC) study aimed to cut headway from up to 4 minutes to 2 minutes — [DNA](https://www.dnaindia.com/mumbai/report-mumbai-s-lifeline-to-get-on-track-with-better-signalling-system-2772095/amp)
- Central Railway runs more than 2,300 local services a day under automatic signalling; most signals are about 400–500 m apart. Red = stop; yellow = next signal red, with the onboard warning repeating until a green or double yellow is seen — [Devdiscourse](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/business/3903622-mumbai-local-trains-get-upgraded-signal-system-for-error-free-operations)
- Mumbai Auxiliary Warning System speed caps: 15 km/h at red, 38 km/h at yellow, 70 km/h at green or double yellow (as extracted; the green figure looks low and may refer to a specific case) — [IRFCA: Mumbai Suburban Auxiliary Warning System](https://irfca.org/docs/mumbai-aws.html) (search extract only)

## 5. Platforms and stations

### Takeaway
Suburban platforms are high-level, raised after a 2014 court direction from the old 760–840 mm range to 840–920 mm above
rail; Western Railway finished raising all 166 of its platforms. Platform lengths in metres were not found. 15-car
extension is a live programme (34 platforms). Thane and Dombivli are extended; Andheri is done; the Bandra–Andheri slow
corridor is to get 15-car trains from January 2027. The Western line had 102 foot overbridges and 8 subways in 2013.

### Cited Findings
- High-level suburban platforms were specified at 760–840 mm; after a 2014 Bombay High Court directive they were raised to 840–920 mm for the Mumbai suburban section, and a later government answer says the raising is done — [Lok Sabha petitions (eparlib)](https://eparlib.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/65556/1/16_Petitions_36.pdf); [Business Standard, Mar 2014](https://www.business-standard.com/article/pti-stories/rly-submits-report-to-hc-on-platform-height-to-avert-mishaps-114031301095_1.html); [eparlib answer](https://eparlib.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/961876/1/AU1473.pdf)

- 34 platforms are to be lengthened for 15-coach trains (MUTP, ₹33,000 crore programme); a Central Railway report says only 5 of 34 stations were ready — [FPJ: 34 platforms](https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/34-mumbai-railway-platforms-to-be-lengthened-for-15-coach-trains-as-part-of-33000-crore-urban-transport-upgrade); [FPJ: only 5 of 34](https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/mumbai-central-railway-platform-extension-work-slow-only-5-of-34-stations-ready-for-15-coach-trains)

## 7. Level crossings and track fencing

### Takeaway
Level crossings are now rare inside Mumbai city and are being removed on the outer Central Railway lines with road
overbridges. Remaining examples in recent news are suburban-fringe gates (Titwala–Khadavli, Diva; Kharegaon is now
closed). Maharashtra's "Railway Fatak Free" plan approves 65 road overbridges at level crossings. No count of remaining gates was found.
