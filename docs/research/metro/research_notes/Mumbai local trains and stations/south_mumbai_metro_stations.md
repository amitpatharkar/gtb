## 1. How deep are the South Mumbai Line 3 stations, how were they built, and how big are the station boxes?

### Takeaway
No station-by-station depth table was found. The sourced picture: 19 (or 20) stations are cut-and-cover boxes "normally up to 250 m long × 22–30 m deep"; seven in congested areas (Hutatma Chowk, Kalbadevi, Girgaon, Grant Road, Shitladevi, Santacruz, Marol Naka) mix cut-and-cover with mined New Austrian Tunnelling Method (NATM) caverns; running tunnels sit at an average 15–25 m depth (TBM cover 15–20 m). Terminal and crossover stations (Cuffe Parade, BKC) are up to 450 m long. The only per-station depth found is CSMIA T2 (26.5 m in Wikipedia's infobox, "about 30 m" elsewhere; called the deepest). Kalbadevi is a mined three-vault station (two 9.45 m platform tunnels + central cavern + two small end boxes); Shitladevi is a 240 m station with one track in a box and the other in a mined tunnel.

### Cited Findings
**Line-wide numbers**
- "Of the 26 underground stations, 19 are cut-and-cover boxes of normally up to 250m long x 22m-30m deep, and seven, in particularly congested areas at Hutatma Chowk, Kalbadevi, Girgaon, Grant Road, Santacruz, Sitladevi and Marol Naka, are a combination of cut-and-cover and mined NATM excavation" (search extract) — [TunnelTalk, 12 Mar 2020](https://www.tunneltalk.com/India-12Mar2020-Mumbai-Metro-Line-3-excavation-methods-advance-and-statistics.php)
- Wikipedia's Aqua Line article instead says the same seven were built by NATM and "the other 20" by cut-and-cover (26 vs 27 underground counts differ by whether Aarey JVLR is counted) (search extract) — [Aqua Line, Wikipedia (via search)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aqua_Line_(Mumbai_Metro))
- A geotechnical paper abstract: "33.5 kilometer of twin tube tunnels by TBM, 19 Cut and Cover Stations, 7 Stations including cross passages and cross overs to be constructed using NATM" (search extract) — [Scribd, "Geotechnical Challenges in Design and Construction of Tunnels for Mumbai Metro Line 3"](https://www.scribd.com/document/593252675/Geotechnical-Challenges-in-Design-and-Construction-of-Tunnels-for-Mumbai-Metro-Line-3)
- Underground construction "at an average depth of 15 to 25 meters" (SYSTRA, whose design package covered Cuffe Parade, Vidhan Bhavan, Churchgate and Hutatma Chowk: "three of the stations are cut-and-cover, and Hutatma Chowk has one tunnel built using NATM") (search extract) — [SYSTRA India, Mumbai Metro Line 3](https://www.systra.com/india/project/mumbai-metro-line-3/)
- TBMs were driven through the NATM station boxes at Kalbadevi, Girgaon and Grant Road "at an average depth of 20m"; at some mined stations (incl. Shitladevi) the segment-lined running tunnels were bored through the station zone first and later broken out for platform enlargements (search extract) — [TunnelTalk](https://www.tunneltalk.com/India-12Mar2020-Mumbai-Metro-Line-3-excavation-methods-advance-and-statistics.php)
- Tunnel rings are typically 1.4 m wide, 5 segments + 1 key segment (search extract) — [The Metro Rail Guy, 2017](https://themetrorailguy.com/2017/03/17/cec-itd-tpl-jv-casts-1st-tunnel-ring-segment-for-mumbais-line-3/); twin bored tunnels have a 5.80 m internal finished diameter (search extract) — [CICI, MM3-CBS-UGC-04](https://www.cici.co.in/index.php/mrts/111-mumbai-metro-mm3-cbs-ugc-04)
- Earlier note (repeated): Cuffe Parade and BKC station boxes are up to 450 m long including crossovers; at Kalbadevi two cut-and-cover boxes "about 50.3 m apart" with the platform section mined between them (search extract) — [Indian Infrastructure, 3 Jul 2026](https://indianinfrastructure.com/2026/07/03/tunnelling-through-complexity-mmrcls-experience-in-executing-indias-first-fully-underground-metro-corridor/)

**Per-station depth (only one found)**
- CSMIA T2: Wikipedia infobox depth 26.5 m (87 ft), one island platform (search extract) — [CSMIA T2 metro station, Wikipedia (via search)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chhatrapati_Shivaji_Maharaj_International_Airport_-_T2_metro_station); also "located approximately 30 metres below ground", and an Indian Express headline calls it the "deepest underground metro station" (search extract, headline only) — same page (conflict 26.5 vs ~30 m; possibly platform vs rail/box bottom)

**Kalbadevi (mined, package 2)**
- Layout: two small cut-and-cover boxes (K1 and K2) for vertical circulation, and "a central cavern connecting two platform tunnels of 9.45m each, excavated by conventional tunnelling"; "Two platforms on UP & DN line tracks and a central horizontal circulation is created in caverns done by Conventional Excavation"; the TBM-lined running tunnels were widened by conventional excavation into platform caverns; the cavern links to the platform tunnels by 6 m and 3 m wide cross passages, 10 cross passages in the station (search extract of the paper's abstract and figure caption) — [Rawat, Gupta, Dalvi, Kumar, "Construction of Kalbadevi underground metro station…", ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/370548457_Construction_of_Kalbadevi_underground_metro_station_of_Mumbai_metro_line_3_by_dealing_with_numerous_social_and_technical_challenges_in_the_congested_old_city_-_financial_capital_of_India); [Taylor & Francis chapter](https://www.taylorfrancis.com/chapters/oa-edit/10.1201/9781003348030-264/construction-kalbadevi-underground-metro-station-mumbai-metro-line-3-dealing-numerous-social-technical-challenges-congested-old-city-financial-capital-india-rawat-gupta-dalvi-kumar); [figure "Kalbadevi station layout"](https://www.researchgate.net/figure/Kalbadevi-station-layout_fig1_370548457)
- MMRC (Jan 2023): Kalbadevi is in package 2; "the tunnel connecting 2 shafts, called Central Cavern & cross passages called adits. These adits pass through one of the hardest rock in Line3 corridor" — [MumbaiMetro3 on X](https://x.com/MumbaiMetro3/status/1613576308352770048)

### Inferences
- **Depth model for the game (estimate, from the cited ranges):** running-tunnel crown 15–20 m below street (TunnelTalk) + 5.8 m inner diameter → rail level ≈ 19–25 m below street; platform floor ≈ rail + 1.1 m ≈ 18–24 m below street. For cut-and-cover boxes 22–30 m deep (to the bottom of the base slab), the same platform depth fits. Use **platform −18 m (shallow boxes, e.g. Cuffe Parade, Vidhan Bhavan, Churchgate, Mumbai Central, Mahalaxmi, Worli, BKC) to −24 m (old-city mined stations: Hutatma Chowk, Kalbadevi, Girgaon, Grant Road)**, concourse slab about −8 to −10 m. None of these per-station values is sourced.
- **Box sizes:** a standard island-platform box ≈ 200–250 m × 22–26 m inside (width estimated: 2 trackways ≈ 2 × 5 m incl. screen-door setback + island 10–12 m + walls ≈ 1 m each), 450 m where crossovers are included (Cuffe Parade, BKC). Dadar's mapped 346 × 41 m suggests a box with a crossover/extra width; treat the mapper's outline as the station's plan footprint.
- **Kalbadevi (and probably Girgaon/Grant Road) should be modelled as a three-vault mined station:** two parallel platform vaults ~9.45 m wide (each = one track + one platform edge), a central cavern between them for circulation, linked by up to 10 cross passages (6 m and 3 m wide), and two small end boxes with escalators/stairs/lifts to the street. "9.45 m" is most likely the finished internal span of each platform tunnel (an inference; the extract does not say span vs diameter).
- Shitladevi: one track in a cut-and-cover box, the other in a mined tunnel → effectively **side platforms**, not an island.

### Gaps
- Per-station platform depth, concourse depth, box length and width for every South Mumbai station: not found (MMRCL station pages, tender drawings and the environmental impact assessment could not be opened).
- Whether Dadar is cut-and-cover (TunnelTalk, Wikipedia) or NATM (CICI): unresolved.
- Girgaon, Grant Road and Hutatma Chowk mined layouts (cavern spans, number of vaults): not found; only Kalbadevi's layout was found.
- The meaning of "50.3 m apart" at Kalbadevi (Indian Infrastructure) does not match the ~125–140 m spacing of the two OSM footprints/entrances; unresolved.

## 2. Levels and platforms: how many levels, island or side platforms, platform length and width, any stacked platforms?

### Takeaway
South Mumbai Line 3 stations are two-level underground stations (street → concourse → platform), each with one island platform per Wikipedia/route sites, built for 8-car trains; OSM-mapped Line 3 platforms (only north of Dharavi) measure 178–186 m long. Exceptions by construction: Kalbadevi has two separate platform tunnels (sources conflict with "island"), Shitladevi has one track in a box and one in a mined tunnel. No source mentions vertically stacked platforms at Kalbadevi or Girgaon.

### Cited Findings
- Cuffe Parade, Mahalaxmi, Jagannath Shankar Sheth (Mumbai Central), Acharya Atre Chowk and CSMT station pages show a ground / concourse / platform layout with one island platform, with no metre figures (search extract) — [Cuffe Parade metro station, Wikipedia (via search)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuffe_Parade_metro_station); [Mahalaxmi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahalaxmi_metro_station); [Jagannath Shankar Sheth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagannath_Shankar_Sheth_metro_station); [Acharya Atre Chowk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acharya_Atre_Chowk_metro_station); [CSMT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chhatrapati_Shivaji_Maharaj_Terminus_metro_station)
- Marol Naka's platform is on the second basement level; the concourse handles ticketing and security (search extract) — [Marol Naka metro station, Wikipedia (via search)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marol_Naka_metro_station)
- Science Centre: one island platform (search extract) — [Science Centre metro station, Wikipedia (via search)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Centre_metro_station)
- Hutatma Chowk, Grant Road, Girgaon, Siddhivinayak: island platform (route sites, search extract) — [YoMetro Hutatma Chowk](https://yometro.com/hutatma-chowk-metro-station-140091); [YoMetro Grant Road](https://yometro.com/grant-road-metro-station-140084); [YoMetro Girgaon](https://yometro.com/girgaon-metro-station-140085); [Wikiwand Siddhivinayak](https://wikiwand.com/en/articles/Siddhivinayak_metro_station)

- Search found no source for stacked (one above the other) platforms at any of Hutatma Chowk, Kalbadevi or Girgaon (search summary) — [TunnelTalk](https://www.tunneltalk.com/India-12Mar2020-Mumbai-Metro-Line-3-excavation-methods-advance-and-statistics.php); [SYSTRA](https://www.systra.com/india/project/mumbai-metro-line-3/)
- Vidhan Bhavan first visuals: "wide platforms", brightly lit, spacious (search extract) — [Curly Tales](https://curlytales.com/india/trending/first-look-of-vidhan-bhavan-metro-station-on-mumbais-aqua-line-unveiled-with-wide-platforms-and-a-modern-design/)
- **OSM platform outlines (Line 3, all north of Dharavi; none mapped from Cuffe Parade to Dharavi):** BKC two areas 180 × 8.7 m and 178 × 8.7 m (`layer=-2`); Bandra Colony 183 × 14.6; Santacruz 185 × 5.6; CSMIA T1 180 × 10.6; Sahar Road 182 × 15.1; CSMIA T2 186 × 21.5; Marol Naka 180 × 10.9; MIDC–Andheri 181 × 6.2; SEEPZ 180 × 7.0; Aarey JVLR two areas 178 × 4.1 and 179 × 5.9 (side platforms, the at-grade terminus) — [osm-metro.json `stations[].platforms`](../../osm-metro.json); BKC: [way 1322610079](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1322610079), [way 1322610112](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1322610112)
- Platform 1 towards Cuffe Parade, Platform 2 towards Aarey JVLR at Siddhivinayak (route site, search extract) — [YoMetro Siddhivinayak](https://yometro.com/siddhivinayak-temple-metro-station-140077)

### Inferences
- **Platform length for the game: 180 m** (all ten mapped Line 3 platforms are 178–186 m; consistent with 8-car trains). Island width: mapped widths vary 5.6–21.5 m because mappers drew different things (platform only, or platform + track bed); **use 10–12 m** for an island (estimate; BKC, T1, Marol Naka cluster at 8.7–10.9 m).
- Two-level stacking for the game: street (0) → concourse (≈ −8 to −10 m; ticket hall, security, fare gates) → platform (≈ −18 to −24 m). Escalator runs per level ≈ 9–12 m rise ≈ 16–21 m horizontal at 30° (geometry, not a source).
- Kalbadevi: build as two side platforms in separate vaults rather than an island, despite the Wikipedia list. Shitladevi: side platforms (one in the box, one in the mined tunnel).

### Gaps
- Island width, concourse length/width, ceiling heights: not found for any station.
- Platform type at Kalbadevi, Girgaon, Grant Road and Hutatma Chowk (mined stations) from an official source: not found.

## 3. Entrances: how many per station, where, and what do they look like at street level (including heritage entrances and ventilation shafts)?

### Takeaway
Entrances are lettered by concourse end (A1, A2… at one end, B1, B2… at the other) and opened in stages; counts per station run from 1–2 mapped (Girgaon, Kalbadevi) to 7 (Siddhivinayak, official). Most entrances are glass-and-steel kiosks over stairs/escalators plus separate lift boxes. Heritage-zone exceptions: Hutatma Chowk's entrances are roofless stone-and-glass wells (only lift shafts and some ventilation structures rise above ground) plus a two-storey entry at Flora Fountain styled after the neighbouring Commissariat Building with a police post upstairs; CSMT has a curved, glass-clad, arched/domed entrance facing the terminus with a lattice roof.

## 7. Future Line 11 (Wadala – Gateway of India): station plan in brief (reference only)

### Takeaway
A fully underground line except one at-grade station, 14 stations, about 17.5 km (an older figure says 14.51 km), from Anik bus depot (Wadala) via Sewri, Byculla, Nagpada, Bhendi Bazaar, Crawford Market, CSMT and Horniman Circle/Kala Ghoda to the Gateway of India; to be built by MMRCL; state-approved September 2025, still awaiting the Centre's approval in September 2026. No station depths were found; nothing is under construction.
