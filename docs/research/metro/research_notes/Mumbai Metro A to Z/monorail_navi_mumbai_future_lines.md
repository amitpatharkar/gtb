## 1. Mumbai Monorail (Line 1, Chembur – Wadala – Sant Gadge Maharaj Chowk / Jacob Circle) — in the game map

### Takeaway
A 17-station, ~19.5–20 km elevated straddle-monorail from Chembur (east) via Wadala to Sant Gadge Maharaj Chowk (Jacob Circle, near Mahalaxmi). It has been SHUT to passengers since 20 September 2025 after the August–September 2025 breakdowns; 10 new Made-in-India Medha–SMH rakes and CBTC signalling got Bureau Veritas and Commissioner of Metro Rail Safety (CMRS) clearance in June 2026, a new private operator (Power Mech) is appointed, but as of the latest reports found (September 2026) no restart date had been announced and no report of a reopening was found up to mid-November 2026. For the game: the line exists in full (beams, stations, depot at Wadala) but in real life it is not running in November 2026.

## 2. Navi Mumbai Metro (summary) and the Navi Mumbai International Airport link

### Takeaway
Navi Mumbai Metro Line 1 (Belapur Terminal – Pendhar, 11.10 km, 11 elevated stations) opened on 17 November 2023, runs about 06:00–22:00 at 10-minute peak / 15-minute off-peak headways, and costs Rs 10–30 since a September 2024 fare cut; it crossed 1 crore (10 million) riders in under two years. No metro reaches Navi Mumbai International Airport (opened to commercial flights 25 December 2025) yet; Navi Mumbai Lines 2–4 and Mumbai Metro Line 8 are planned links.

## 3. Future and partly opened Mumbai Metro lines (status table)

After the April 2026 openings of Line 2B phase 1 and Line 9 phase 1, roughly 91–101 km of metro runs in Mumbai (sources differ), against a ~337 km master plan. Next openings (targets, not confirmed): Line 4/4A Gaimukh–Cadbury Junction (Thane) and Line 9 phase 2 to Bhayandar in December 2026, Line 5 phase 1 Thane–Bhiwandi and Line 6 Pink Line "second half of 2026". ### Inferences
- Rough check of the operational total: Lines 1, 2A, 7, 3 (~80 km) + 2B phase 1 (~5.5 km) + 9 phase 1 (~5.6 km) ≈ 91 km, which matches Wikipedia's 91.48 km; the 101.4 km figure may count something else (for example the monorail or a stretch not confirmed here). Monorail (~19.5 km, suspended) and Navi Mumbai Line 1 (11.1 km) are separate systems.
- Inside the current game map (Colaba to Bandra/Andheri/Kurla/Powai): Monorail (all of it), Line 11 (future, all underground, Wadala–Gateway), Line 2B (Bandra–BKC–Kurla–Chembur), Line 6 (Andheri–JVLR–Powai area–Vikhroli), Line 4 (Wadala–Ghatkopar–Mulund, southern end) and Line 8 (near the Mumbai airport end). Lines 5, 9, 10, 12, 13, 14 and Navi Mumbai are outside it.
- For the game, every "expected date" above has slipped at least once (monorail: May → monsoon → September → Diwali → December 2026; Lines 2B/9: December 2025 → April 2026), so treat them as targets, not facts.
