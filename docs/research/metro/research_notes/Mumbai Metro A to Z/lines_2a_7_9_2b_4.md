# Mumbai Metro Lines 2A, 7, 9, 2B and 4/4A — fact record as of November 2026

Research date: 2026-11-14. Method note: in this cloud session the page fetcher could not reach en.wikipedia.org, wikiwand.com or freepressjournal.in (DNS blocked), so every finding below comes from search-engine result snippets and summaries of the cited pages (MMRDA official pages, Free Press Journal, Mid-day-class news sites, Wikipedia, aggregators). Numbers quoted are as the snippets gave them; anything not confirmed is in "Gaps". Where sources disagree, both are given.

## 1. Stations in order, length, geometry, roads and interchanges (per line)

### Takeaway
All five lines are fully elevated except the airport spur 7A (partly underground). Line 2A = 17 stations / 18.6 km; Line 7 = 14 stations incl. shared Dahisar East / 16.5 km; Line 9 = 8 stations / ~10.6 km (Dahisar East–Subhash Chandra Bose Stadium, Bhayandar); Line 2B = 20 stations / 23.643 km (DN Nagar–Mandale); Line 4 = 30–32 stations / 32.32 km (Wadala–Kasarvadavali) plus 4A = 2 stations / 2.7 km to Gaimukh. Official per-station chainage could not be retrieved; only averages can be derived.

### Inferences
- Average spacing (derived): 2A ≈ 18.6 km / 16 gaps ≈ 1.16 km; Line 7 ≈ 16.5 / 13 ≈ 1.27 km; Line 9 ≈ 10.6 / 7 ≈ 1.5 km; Line 9 Phase 1 ≈ 4.7 / 3 ≈ 1.6 km; 2B ≈ 23.6 / 20 ≈ 1.2 km; Line 4 ≈ 32.3 / 31 ≈ 1.05 km. For a 1:1 game map, placing stations from OpenStreetMap `railway=station`/`station=subway|light_rail` nodes will be more accurate than any table found here.
- Roads (from the brief and general knowledge, not re-verified this session): Line 2A runs above New Link Road (Link Road) from Dahisar to D N Nagar; Line 7 runs above the Western Express Highway; Line 9 continues north along the Western Express Highway / Mira–Bhayandar road; Line 2B runs SV Road–BKC–Kurla–Sion–Trombay Road; Line 4 runs over LBS Marg and the Eastern Express Highway, then Ghodbunder Road in Thane (the 4A Ghodbunder alignment is confirmed by MMRDA).
- Dahisar East is the junction of the whole northern network (2A, 7, 9) and is the obvious "hub" set-piece for a game.

## 2. Operational status in November 2026 and opening dates

### Takeaway
Open to passengers in November 2026: all of Line 2A (since 2 Apr 2022 / 19 Jan 2023), all of Line 7 (same dates), Line 9 Phase 1 Dahisar East–Kashigaon (inaugurated 7 Apr 2026, public from 8 Apr), Line 2B Mandale–Diamond Garden (7/8 Apr 2026) plus Chembur station (13 Aug 2026). NOT open: Line 4/4A (Gaimukh–Cadbury Junction targeted December 2026, rest 2027), Line 9 Phase 2 (December 2026 target), Line 2B west/central sections, Line 7A airport spur.

## 3. Operator, depots and rolling stock
