## 2. Everyday life to stage

- **Dabbawalas** (~5,000 carry ~200,000 lunches (?)): white caps, coded lids, crates on heads at Churchgate 11:30–12:30. *Game:* lunchtime relay; decode lids.
- **Local trains:** Western (Churchgate–Virar/Dahanu), Central (CSMT–Kasara/Khopoli), Harbour (CSMT–Panvel/Goregaon); ~7 million riders a day. Ladies' and first-class coaches, door hangers, the brutal "Virar fast", sealed AC locals, hawkers, bhajan groups. *Game:* "Utarne ka hai?" door scrum; a man in the ladies' coach gets scolded and fined; NPCs hang from doors, the player cannot.
- **Metro 3:** underground fast travel. **Monorail:** stuck-train crane rescue.
- **BEST buses:** conductor, bell cord; red electric double-deckers since 2023. *Game:* top-deck sightseeing; bus-driver job.
- **Taxis and autos:** "Nahi jaayega" refusals, "Chhutta do"; autos only north of Bandra/Sion. *Game:* refusal comedy.
- **Street food:** vada pav, pav bhaji, bhel, pani puri, cutting chai, Bombay sandwich (green chutney, potato, beet), kulfi, falooda, misal, ice gola, corn and onion bhajji in rain. *Game:* hunger buffs by hour and district.
- **Monsoon (June–September):** flooding at Hindmata (19.0095, 72.8420) and Milan subway (~19.0790, 72.8420 (?)); alerts for tides above ~4.5 m; umbrellas, plastic-covered bikes, potholes. *Game:* flood zones when rain meets high tide; push-start stalled cars.
- **Festivals:** Jan: Marathon (CSMT, Sea Link, Coastal Road), Sankranti kites. Feb: Kala Ghoda. Mar/Apr: Holi; Gudi Padwa processions in Girgaon. Ramzan nights and Eid on Mohammed Ali Road shift ~11 days earlier yearly. Aug: Narali Purnima (Kolis offer coconuts to the sea), Dahi Handi pyramids of 9–10 tiers (?). Aug/Sep: Ganesh Chaturthi, immersions at Girgaum Chowpatty and Juhu. Sep: Mount Mary fair. Sep/Oct: Navratri garba. Oct/Nov: Diwali lanterns. Dec: Bandra Christmas, Mahim fair, New Year's Eve on Marine Drive. *Game:* event calendar that reroutes traffic.
- **Cricket:** overlapping maidan games, gully tennis-ball cricket, Kanga League in July rain (since 1948). *Game:* stray balls hit the taxi; join in.
- **Koli fisherfolk:** koliwadas at Worli, Colaba, Versova, Mahim; nauvari saris, baskets on heads, monsoon fishing ban. *Game:* bargaining aunties, fish runs.
- **Street shoots:** bouncers stop traffic, junior artists, vanity vans. *Game:* random road block; extra job.
- **Hoardings:** topical-pun parody ads about the player ("Challan Champion!").
- **Traffic habits:** constant horns ("Horn OK Please"), lane straddling, filtering two-wheelers, cows. **Also:** "Paper raddi!" scrap callers, watchmen on plastic chairs.
